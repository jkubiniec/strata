# Eleven moving worlds · film credits

These eleven silent excerpts are the sources for the Strata multi-video flight. Each file contains one motion sequence at 480 × 270 and 20 fps. Times below are offsets in the source movies. The solar and ocean sequences started as scientific imagery. The other nine show footage from orbit, land, or a remotely operated underwater vehicle.

Visualization by Tom Bridgman. | 00:15–00:55, 5× | SDO’s Atmospheric Imaging Assembly photographed glowing plasma at 304 ångströms. The orange is assigned to that extreme-ultraviolet channel. |

Orbital videos courtesy of the Earth Science and Remote Sensing Unit, NASA Johnson Space Center. The orbital time-lapses are assembled from astronaut photographs. NASA describes reuse and its preferred credit in its [astronaut imagery FAQ](https://eol.jsc.nasa.gov/FAQ/). NASA’s Scientific Visualization Studio describes its visualizations as public domain unless an item notes an exception. The sample package contains only the silent visuals. [SVS usage guidance](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/).

NOAA identifies public-domain video from its Ocean Exploration program and requests attribution. The USGS source record marks the Kīlauea footage public domain. [NOAA media kit](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/about/media-kit/) · [USGS source record](https://www.usgs.gov/media/videos/december-24-2024-video-lava-fountains-kilauea-summit).

Excerpts were cropped and downsampled for browser playback. The prepared aurora, lava, Milky Way, Nile, Africa, and sea cucumber excerpts have modest saturation or exposure adjustments. Source records link to the original presentations.
